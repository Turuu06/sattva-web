Frequently asked questions

Where can I get the app?

It’s coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. We’ll add the links here as soon as it’s out.

How do I sign in?

Enter your 8-digit phone number and we’ll text you a 6-digit code. Never share it — we will never ask for it by phone or message. You can try free meditations without signing in.

What’s free, and what does membership add?

Open meditations, the timer, your schedule, the first 10 journey steps and Seva are free. Membership adds all video lectures and books, journey step 11 onwards, and offline downloads. Price to be announced. Course practices open to people who have completed that course.

Where does my donation go?

All donations go to the Art of Living LLC account and are spent on the project you choose. Every project shows progress and updates; completed projects have a report. Donating in the app is coming soon.

How do I register for a course?

Choose a programme in the Courses tab and tap “Register”. If dates aren’t announced yet you’ll see “Dates coming soon” — you can ask for a reminder.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile › Settings › Delete account. Without the app, send a request at sattva.mn/delete-account.
